About YSA

Where young scientists change the world

Founded in 2017 by Dr. Rob Condon, the Young Scientist Academy has grown from a single program in Wilmington, NC to an international network spanning four countries.

"Everyone's a scientist — they simply don't know it yet."

YSA provides hands-on programs that teach students how to apply the scientific method and think critically about real-world solutions. By immersing students in authentic research experiences, we build not just science skills but identity, agency, and a lifelong relationship with discovery.

Our Story
2017

YSA Founded

Dr. Rob Condon launches the Young Scientist Academy in Wilmington, NC, bringing hands-on STEM experiences to Title I schools and underserved communities.

2021

First Youth STEM Summit

Launch of the annual Youth STEM Summit, creating a platform for student researchers to present original fieldwork to peers and community leaders.

2023

Moldova Exchange Program

YSA launches its international partnership with Moldova, connecting student teams from Chișinău and surrounding communities with NC Ambassadors for shared fieldwork and joint publications.

2026

First Joint In-Person Summit

NC Ambassadors travel to Moldova's Codrii Reserve for the 5th Annual Youth STEM Summit — the first time both cohorts share the same field, the same stage, and the same weather balloon launch.

Programs

Built for every science journey

From first experiments to international research conferences — YSA has a track for every student.

🌐 Advanced Track

Global STEM Ambassador Program

YSA's flagship year-long research and leadership program. Ambassador teams conduct original field research, produce formal presentations, and represent their communities at international events.

Since 2017, Ambassadors from North Carolina have partnered with schools and youth organizations across the U.S. and abroad. In 2023, YSA launched its Moldova exchange — connecting teams from Chișinău with NC peers for shared fieldwork and joint publications.

  • Year-long commitment with weekly meetings
  • Original field research and formal presentations
  • International summit participation
  • Mentorship from scientists and community leaders
  • Application-based · Explorer track preferred
🔬 Entry Track

STEM Explorer Program

YSA's entry-level program for students starting their science journey. Explorers build foundational skills, run hands-on experiments, and discover what STEM looks like in the real world.

After completing the Explorer program, students may apply to the Global STEM Ambassador Program to continue their science career with YSA.

  • Open to all students, no prior experience required
  • Hands-on experiments and field activities
  • Summer STEM workshops and community events
  • Clear pathway to Ambassador track
  • Welcoming · Beginner-friendly · Community-focused
