Code Green

Tracking green space coverage in urban Wilmington, NC from 2019–2025 using real NASA/USGS Landsat 8 and Sentinel-2 satellite data. Visualized on a live interactive map with year-by-year comparison.

Research hypothesis: COVID-19 lockdowns paused construction in 2020, creating a backlog that unleashed an accelerated post-pandemic development surge in 2021–2024. Wilmington's developed land expanded by nearly half in a single year (2022).
