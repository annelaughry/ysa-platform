Project Amoeba

Design the next YSA mascot. The amoeba adapts to every environment — just like our scientists adapt to new challenges. We want something bold, collaborative, and unmistakably YSA.

📅  Submissions open — deadline TBD
The Rules

What makes a winning design?

We want something you made yourself — no shortcuts. Here's what we're judging on:

🏻
No AI Generation
Hand-drawn or digital — both are welcome. The design must come from your own hands and imagination, not a generator. We'll ask you to confirm this on submission.
📈
Scalable
Your design must work at any size — from a phone screen to a banner. Vector-friendly or clean enough to be vectorized. Test it small and large before you submit.
🨀
Core Amoeba Shape
The design needs a recognizable dome/core that builds outward. The amoeba adapts to its environment — show us how it takes shape while staying true to itself.

What is the amoeba concept?

An amoeba has no fixed form — it moves, adapts, and takes shape around whatever it encounters. That's exactly what YSA scientists do. The mascot should feel like it's always in motion, always curious, always growing.

The vibe we're going for: somewhere between a trendy golf clubhouse and a science lab. Collaborative, innovative, a place you want to hang out. Not a lone wolf — something that thrives in community.
