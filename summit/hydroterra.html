Context

Why It Matters

Moldova's water infrastructure reflects decades of underinvestment. Aging Soviet-era pipes in urban centers leach lead and corrode in ways that standard utility reports don't capture at the granularity needed for timely public health decisions. Chisinau residents often have no way to know whether their tap water meets safe thresholds on any given day.

In rural areas, the situation is more acute. Millions of Moldovans rely on private wells that have never been formally tested. Agricultural runoff from surrounding farmland carries nitrates and ammonia directly into shallow groundwater, and bacterial contamination from livestock operations is common but largely invisible to the communities it affects most.
