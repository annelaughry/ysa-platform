About the Summit

Where young scientists change the world

Join the Young Scientist Academy for our 5th Annual Youth STEM Summit, the first time NC ambassadors are joining Moldova in person for a joint summit. Students from North Carolina and Moldova come together to present their research, conduct field experiments, and launch weather balloons to the edge of space.

This event is structured as a scientific conference where students from multiple countries and languages coalesce and cross-pollinate innovative solutions to real-world problems.

🌐 Global STEM Ambassador Program
▶ About this program

The Global STEM Ambassador Program is YSA's advanced research and leadership track — a year-long commitment where student teams conduct original field research, produce formal presentations, and represent their communities at international events.

Since 2017, Ambassadors from North Carolina have partnered with schools and youth organizations across the U.S. and abroad. In 2023, YSA launched its Moldova exchange, connecting teams from Chișinău and surrounding communities with their American peers for shared fieldwork and joint publications.

This year's summit marks the first time NC Ambassadors have traveled to Moldova to work alongside their Moldovan counterparts in person — 10 teams, 3 countries, one summit.

Discover Moldova

A hidden gem at the heart of Europe — ancient forests, world-class wine, and a scientific legacy waiting to be shared.

🌲

Codrii Reserve

Protected old-growth forest in the geographic center of Moldova — home to rare wildlife, deer herds, and the research site for our summit.

A special thank you to Dr. Gheorghe Manic, Director of Codrii Reserve, for welcoming YSA and making this summit possible.

🍷

Cricova

Home to the world's largest wine collection — over 1.5 million bottles in underground galleries stretching 200km.

🏛️

Chisinau

Moldova's capital — a city of Soviet architecture, green parks, and a growing youth science community.
