Design Competition

🎨 Project Amoeba

YSA is launching a design competition for our new mascot concept. The amoeba adapts to its environment, just like our scientists adapt to new challenges. We want something bold, scalable, and unmistakably YSA.

Think trendy golf clubhouse meets science lab. Collaborative vibes. A place where people want to hang out and do great things together.

Submit Design →
  • 🚫
    No AI ArtDesigns must be original. Hand-drawn, digital, mixed media all welcome. AI-generated images will be disqualified.
  • 💥
    Core ShapeMust have a dome-shaped core section it builds outward from. Think: amoeba in its natural state, then expanding.
  • 📈
    ScalableMust work at any size. From phone screen to banner. Vector-friendly or clean enough to vectorize.
  • 🧪
    YSA VibesInnovative, fun, collaborative. It should feel like a place people want to be. Bold but approachable.
  • 🌟
    AdaptableThe logo should feel like it can build into different contexts as the amoeba takes different shapes.
  • 🎉
    Have FunThis is a real competition with real stakes. But also: be weird, be bold, think outside the dome.

Our Story

Founded in Wilmington. Growing worldwide.

In 2017, the Young Scientist Academy was founded in Wilmington, NC by Dr. Rob Condon. A university science professor with over 20 years of experience, Dr. Condon saw that the standard curriculum prioritized memorization over real problem-solving. He believed even elementary and middle schoolers could execute advanced research if given the chance.

YSA started with about 10 students per week. That first group launched YSA's very first weather balloon, taking science to new heights. Since then, YSA students have tackled ionic thrusters, urban heat island mapping, water quality dashboards, and atmospheric science.

In 2023, YSA expanded internationally through the NC-Moldova Bilateral Partnership. In October 2025, YSA launched its highest-level initiative: the Global STEM Ambassadors program, connecting youth researchers across North Carolina, Moldova, Yemen, and Syria.

Today, YSA serves over 350 youth per week during the school year, and the ambassador cohort continues to grow.
